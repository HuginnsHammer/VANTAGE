HOW WE THINK

Security needs
better questions.

VANTAGE is being built around a few non-negotiable principles that shape every assessment and every product decision.

01

Evidence first

A finding must be traceable to an observation. Uncertainty should be visible, never hidden.

02

Permission always

Assessment scope and authorization are explicit. No arbitrary targets, no surprise active testing.

03

Context matters

Severity alone is not business risk. Asset criticality, confidence and exposure all matter.

04

Closure is proven

Incomplete scans do not silently clear findings. Resolution requires a trustworthy observation.
